# R1. Problem, Questions and Goals

## The problem
The district runs a free, voluntary after-school tutoring program. Students
referred by teachers, counselors, parents or themselves, and they get a weekly 45-minute
session in Mathematics, English Language Arts or Science, in groups of up to
four. At the end of the year, students who were tutored failed
more than students who were not. A simple comparison suggests the
program does not work, and administrators are considering cutting its funding.

## The decision
Should the district keep, change, or cut funding for the tutoring program?

We do not assume the program works or fails. We test whether the simple
comparison is fair.

## Questions and goals

| # | Question | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Were the tutored students already struggling before tutoring?| To check if they were failing because of their past grades, not because of tutoring. |
| Q2 | Did referred students actually attend their tutoring sessions? | To find out how many students really attended, not just signed up. |
| Q3 | Do bus riders miss tutoring more often? | To see if having no late bus stops students from attending. |
| Q4 | If two students had similar grades before, does the one who goes to tutoring more often do better? | To make a fair comparison to see if tutoring really helps. |
